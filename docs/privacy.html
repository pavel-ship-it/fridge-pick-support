Privacy Policy

Fridge Pick (iOS and Android) Effective date: 27 October 2026

Fridge Pick is developed by Pavel Yakimenko ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app does not access the internet at all: it makes no network requests and sends nothing anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the products you mark in "My fridge", your dish history (when you last ate each dish), dishes you add, your weekly plan, shopping list checkmarks, and your allergy and kitchen settings. This data is stored only locally on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else, and I have no access to it.

Your allergy settings are used only on your device to mark and optionally hide dishes. They are not transmitted anywhere.

Your device's own backup features (for example, iCloud or Google device backup) may include app data, depending on your system settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No internet access. The app makes no network requests of any kind, so nothing is sent from it. All dishes, recipes and photos are built into the app.

Crash reports

The app does not include any crash reporting service. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may provide me with anonymous crash reports through their own systems. This is controlled by you in your system settings and governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because I do not collect or process your personal data, I hold no data about you to access, correct, export or delete. You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app. If you have any questions about your privacy, contact me at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
