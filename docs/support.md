# Support

Hungry Dishes shows quick dishes for when you're hungry, with the ones you haven't eaten in a while at the top. It includes recipes, nutrition info, allergen markers, a weekly plan and a shopping list. No account is needed, and everything you enter stays on your device.

**Contact the developer:** [dreamcomenull@gmail.com](mailto:dreamcomenull@gmail.com)
I reply to every message as soon as I can.

### FAQ

**How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?**
Email [dreamcomenull@gmail.com](mailto:dreamcomenull@gmail.com). Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, the app version (shown in the App Store or Google Play), and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

**Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?**
Your dish history, weekly plan, shopping list checkmarks, allergy and kitchen settings are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and never sends this data anywhere. In the app you can clear the weekly plan ("Clear week") and change your allergy and kitchen settings at any time. To delete all app data at once, uninstall the app (on Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Hungry Dishes → Storage → Clear storage).

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. All dishes, recipes and photos are built into the app, so it works fully without an internet connection. You only need internet to install and update the app.

**Can I rely on the allergen labels?**
No, please don't rely on them alone. Allergen markers and warnings are a convenience based on typical recipes, and they may be incomplete. Ingredients and brands vary, so always check the actual ingredients and product labels. The app is not medical advice. If you have a serious allergy, follow your doctor's guidance.

**Where do the dish photos come from?**
Most photos are from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their Creative Commons licenses. The rest are from Unsplash. Authors and licenses are listed on the [Photo Credits]({{ '/credits' | relative_url }}) page.
