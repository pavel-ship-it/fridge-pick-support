1. Getting started

Install Fridge Pick from the App Store or Google Play and open it. There is nothing to sign up for, and the app works without an internet connection.

  1. On first launch you see Welcome. The first step asks Any food allergies? Tap every allergen you avoid, or tap None, then Next. Skip leaves that step as it is.
  2. The next step is Your kitchen & tastes. You can change appliances later in Settings → Kitchen.
  3. The last step is What’s in your fridge? Tick what you have, then tap Start cooking. Skip keeps going without saving that step.

You land on Home, which suggests what to cook from what’s at home. A good next step, if you skipped the fridge, is to open Fridge and tick what you have. You can change allergies, the kitchen and the fridge any time in Settings.

A glass tab bar floats along the bottom, the same on iPhone and Android. The screen shows through the blurred bar. The tabs are Home, Cookbook, Fridge and Plan. On a Russian phone they read Главная, Книга, Холодильник and План. Fridge opens My fridge. Plan opens the week plan. On Home, a list button opens the Cookbook and a gear opens Settings. The round I’m hungry button floats on the Cookbook, above the tab bar.

2. Home

Home is the screen the app opens on. The title is Fridge Pick. It answers what you can cook from what’s at home. It is not the full list; that is the Cookbook.

Outlined chips along the top narrow the picks. They combine.

  • Quick: about 5 minutes or less. On a Russian phone, Быстро.
  • Hearty: 400 kcal or more. In Russian, Сытно.
  • Sweet. In Russian, Сладкое.
  • No stove: a method that does not need a hob or oven. In Russian, Без плиты.

When you have ticked products, a row called In the fridge (В холодильнике in Russian) shows them as peach chips. They start checked. Leave a chip checked to keep dishes that use one of those products. Clear every chip and that narrowing goes away. The row is hidden while the fridge is empty.

The heading follows the clock:

  • 05:00–10:59: Good morning · breakfast (Доброе утро · завтрак).
  • 11:00–15:59: Good afternoon · lunch (Добрый день · обед).
  • 16:00–21:59: Good evening · dinner (Добрый вечер · ужин).
  • Otherwise: Good night · a light snack (Доброй ночи · перекус).

One large card is the pick: the photo, the name, and a short line. A green Can make now badge (Можно сейчас in Russian) sits on the photo when the fridge covers the dish. The line under the name is the time, or From what you have when you can make it. When the season is why this dish is ahead of the next one, that line reads In season instead (По сезону on a Russian phone). If the dish accepts any of several fruits and you have one, the line names it, for example With banana from the fridge (С бананом из холодильника).

→ Cook this (→ Готовлю) opens the recipe. Other (Другое) moves on and does not repeat a dish already shown this visit. Up to two smaller cards sit under the hero. Whole cookbook → (Вся поваренная книга →) opens the Cookbook.

Below that, two columns, each hidden when empty and showing at most six dishes: Haven’t cooked lately (Давно не готовил), for dishes you have cooked before and not in the last 5 days, and Favorites (Любимое).

Home skips dishes you cooked today or yesterday. It also leaves out hidden dishes and dishes that don’t suit your allergies, excluded ingredients or kitchen. A dish is not hidden just for being out of season; the season only changes what comes first. The hemisphere comes from the phone’s region. A region mostly south of the equator, such as Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Argentina, Chile or Brazil, uses the opposite season. Countries on the equator, and a region the app does not recognise, stay on the northern calendar: winter is December to February, spring March to May, summer June to August, and autumn September to November.

If nothing is left, Home says “Nothing fits. Clear a filter or open the cookbook.” When you have already passed every pick, it says “That’s every pick for now.” An empty menu says “No dishes on your menu.” On a Russian phone those lines are “Ничего не подходит. Снимите фильтр или откройте книгу.”, “Это все варианты на сейчас.” and “В меню пока пусто.”

3. Cookbook and dish cards

The Cookbook tab (Книга in Russian) is the full list of dishes, as photo cards. The title is Fridge Pick. Dishes you have never made come first, followed by the ones you haven’t had for the longest time, so the grid keeps your meals varied.

Each card can show:

  • Name and time, for example “~8 min”.
  • When you last had it, once you have cooked it: “today” or “3 days ago”. A dish you have never cooked leaves this line blank.
  • A badge only if you cooked it in the last 14 days, including day 14: Cooked today, Cooked yesterday or Cooked 3 days ago. On a Russian phone: Готовили сегодня, Готовили вчера or Готовили 3 дня назад. There is no badge for a dish you have never cooked, or last cooked longer ago than that. A dish you cooked before, and not in the last 5 days, is tinted instead.
  • A fridge hint once you’ve ticked products in My fridge: Can make now, Missing: tomato or You have 4/7.
  • Allergen markers. If a dish contains one of your allergens it gets a red border and a warning badge.
  • A small heart if it’s one of your favorites.

Tap a card to open the dish page. Long-press a card for quick actions: Like / Unlike and Hide from menu. After hiding you can tap Undo; later, restore it in Settings → Menu. The I’m hungry button floats above the tab bar.

4. Filters

The row of chips on the Cookbook filters the grid. Scroll it sideways to see all of them. Filters combine: for example, Favorites + Quick shows only favorite dishes that take about 5 minutes.

  • Can make now: only dishes you can cook with what’s in My fridge. While your fridge is empty this chip reads From my fridge and opens My fridge so you can set it up.
  • Safe for me (appears once you set allergies): hides dishes with your allergens. When it’s on, the chip shows how many dishes are hidden. Turn it off to see them dimmed with a warning.
  • Without excluded (appears once you exclude an ingredient): hides dishes that need an excluded ingredient.
  • Favorites: only the dishes you gave a heart. If you haven’t liked any yet, the app tells you to tap the heart on a dish.
  • Quick: dishes that take about 5 minutes or less.
  • Seasonal (По сезону on a Russian phone): only dishes tagged for the current season. All-year dishes, and dishes tagged for another season, stay off this chip. The season follows the phone’s region, the same way as In season on Home.
  • Not eaten lately: dishes you have never made or haven’t made for 5 days or more.
  • Eggs, Dairy, Pantry, Produce, Protein: food groups. Select one or more to see dishes from any of them.

If no dish matches, tap Clear filters.

5. My fridge

My fridge is the list of products you have at home. Open it with the Fridge tab, or Settings → My fridge. The screen title stays My fridge. Fridge Pick compares it with each dish’s ingredients to show what you can cook right now.

How to fill your fridge

  1. Open the Fridge tab (or Settings → My fridge).
  2. Tap the products you have. They are grouped as Eggs, Dairy, Produce, Protein, Pantry, Other and Fruits. A tick means you have it. On a Russian phone the Fruits heading is Фрукты. The other headings stay in English.
  3. Can’t find something? Type it into Search or add a product. If it isn’t in the list, tap Add “…” to add your own product.
  4. The summary at the top shows how many products you have and how many dishes you can make now.

Fruits

There is no generic Fruits item. The Fruits section has its own chips: Apple, Banana, Orange, Lemon, Lime, Pear, Berries, Strawberry, Blueberry, Grapes, Kiwi, Mango, Pineapple, Melon, Watermelon and Peach. On a Russian phone those chips are Яблоко, Банан, Апельсин, Лимон, Лайм, Груша, Ягоды, Клубника, Голубика, Виноград, Киви, Манго, Ананас, Дыня, Арбуз and Персик. Avocado stays under Produce. Dried fruits stay under Pantry.

A recipe that names one fruit needs that fruit. A recipe that accepts several, such as fruit salad or yoghurt with fruit, is happy with any one of them. On the dish page that kind of line reads Any fruit. Berries also matches a strawberry or a blueberry. Fruit salad still needs lemon, and berries stay an optional extra on that dish. Melon with feta accepts melon or watermelon.

If you used to tick Fruits. The next time you open the app, a ticked Fruits item becomes Apple, Banana, Orange, Berries, Grapes, Melon and Watermelon, and only that old tick is removed. Pear, Peach, Kiwi, Mango, Pineapple, Strawberry and Blueberry are not filled in. Lemon, lime, avocado, dried fruit and every other product stay as they were. If you never ticked Fruits, nothing is added.

An old exclusion of Fruits still blocks a dish that accepts any fruit, including melon with feta. It does not block a dish that names one fruit, or only berries. A shopping-list line that still said Fruits becomes Any fruit.

Staples. Salt, pepper, oil and water are treated as always at home. If you run out of one, tap it in the Staples group to mark it as missing.

Optional extras, like a few herbs on top, never count as missing.

Start over. Clear all unticks every product (staples are kept). Tap Undo if you did it by mistake.

You can also tick ingredients on any dish page; they are saved to My fridge too.

6. Dish page

Tap any dish to open its page. From top to bottom you’ll find:

  • Photo and quick facts: time, the cooking method chosen for your kitchen (for example “Stovetop · no microwave” or “Steamed · steam over oven”), when you last had it, how many times you made it, and food-group tags.
  • Warnings if the dish contains one of your allergens or needs an ingredient you excluded.
  • Ingredients with checkboxes and a counter such as You have 3/5 or You have everything. A line that accepts any of several fruits reads Any fruit, and ticking one of them is enough. Amounts, oven temperatures and lengths follow Units.
  • Allergens of the dish. Optional extras that may add an allergen are listed separately.
  • Nutrition (per serving): approximate calories, protein, carbs and fat.
  • Recipe: numbered steps for the method that fits your kitchen.

Tick what you have and shop for the rest

  1. Tap an ingredient to tick it. Ticks are saved to My fridge.
  2. Tap the cart icon next to a missing ingredient to add it to your shopping list. It then shows On list.
  3. Missing more than one? Tap Add N missing to shopping list to add them all at once. Tap Open in the message to jump to the list.

Mark it as made

When you’ve cooked it, tap I made this at the bottom. The dish is marked as eaten today. On the Cookbook it sorts with the dishes you had most recently, and a Cooked today badge appears. Fridge Pick uses this to suggest things you haven’t had lately.

More actions

  • Heart (top right): add to or remove from Favorites.
  • Share (top right): send a recipe card.
  • ⋮ menu: Exclude an ingredient… and Hide from menu / Unhide.
  • Long-press an ingredient to Never use it. See Excluded ingredients.

7. Sharing a recipe card

Send any dish to a friend as a good-looking picture card, together with the recipe as text.

  1. Open a dish and tap the share icon (Share recipe) at the top.
  2. Your phone’s share sheet opens. Pick a messenger, email, Notes, or save the image.

The card shows the dish photo and name, time, cooking method, calories, nutrition per serving (approximate), all ingredients and all steps. A long recipe simply makes a taller card, so nothing is cut off. At the bottom it says Fridge Pick: Quick Meal Ideas with a link to this website and credits the photo’s author and license.

The recipe steps follow your kitchen settings, and the amounts follow Units, just like on the dish page.

8. Favorites

  1. Tap the heart on a dish page, or long-press a card on the Cookbook and choose Like.
  2. Liked dishes get a small red heart on their card.
  3. Turn on the Favorites filter on the Cookbook to see only them. Home also lists favorites in their own column.

Favorites also count in I’m hungry: dishes you love get a boost and show “You love this”. To remove a favorite, tap the heart again or choose Unlike.

9. I’m hungry

Not sure what to eat? Tap I’m hungry on the Cookbook, above the tab bar, and answer up to four quick questions. Tap one answer, or Skip any question.

  1. How much time do you have? 5 min, 15 min or No rush.
  2. How hungry are you? Just a snack (roughly under 300 kcal) or Proper meal (300 kcal or more).
  3. What are you craving? Sweet, Savory, Protein-rich, Something light or Anything.
  4. Use what you have? Choose Use My fridge to put dishes you can make right now first, or pick food groups you have at hand. Then tap Show dishes.

You get 3–5 suggestions under Try one of these, each with a short reason, such as “You have everything”, “You love this”, “Haven’t had it in 9 days” or “Ready in 5 min”. Tap one to open its recipe. Show other options gives you new ideas, and Start over asks the questions again.

Suggestions never include hidden dishes, dishes with your allergens, dishes that need an excluded ingredient, or dishes you can’t cook with your kitchen appliances. If nothing matches perfectly, the closest matches are shown and marked as such.

10. Week plan

Plan meals for the next 7 days, starting today, with up to 3 dishes a day. Days can also stay empty.

Fill the week automatically

  1. Tap Plan on the tab bar to open Week plan. On a Russian phone the tab is План.
  2. Tap Fill week. Each tap adds one more dish to every day that has room.
  3. Tap again for a second or third dish per day.

Fill week keeps your existing picks, never puts the same dish twice on one day, spreads dishes across the week, and prefers dishes you haven’t had lately. It only uses dishes that suit your allergies, excluded ingredients and kitchen, and never uses hidden dishes.

Add or replace a dish by hand

  • Tap + next to a day to Add a dish. The list is sorted by least recently eaten.
  • Tap × next to a dish to remove it. Fridge Pick offers three replacements for that day, each with a hint such as “Can make now” or “Never tried”. Tap one to use it, Shuffle for three more, Undo to put the dish back, or No thanks.

Clear the week

Open the ⋮ menu and choose Clear week to remove all planned dishes for the 7 days.

Automatic clean-up. If you later hide a planned dish, add an allergy or exclude an ingredient, the affected dishes are taken off the plan the next time you open it. A banner tells you how many were removed; tap Suggest replacements to fill the gaps or Keep empty.

11. Shopping list

The shopping list is built for you from your week plan, plus anything you added from dish pages. Open it with the cart icon in Week plan, or tap Open after adding an ingredient on a dish page.

  • Added by you: ingredients you added from dish pages. Tap × to remove one.
  • For planned dishes: every ingredient your planned dishes need, listed once, with how many dishes use it.
  • Optional extras: things that are optional in every dish that uses them. Buy them only if you want.
  • Already in fridge: products you already have in My fridge, set aside so you don’t buy them twice.

Ingredients from dishes with your allergens and ingredients you excluded are never put on the list. If a line has alternatives, like “Butter or oil”, and you excluded butter, the list just says “Oil”.

Use it in the store

  1. Tick items as you put them in your basket. The top line shows how many are checked.
  2. Tap Reset to untick everything for the next trip.

Share the list

Tap the share icon (Share list) at the top. Fridge Pick sends the items you haven’t ticked yet as a text checklist (with optional extras in a separate block), ready to paste into a messenger or notes app.

12. Settings: Menu and your own dishes

Open Settings with the gear on Home or the Cookbook, and tap Menu to see every dish in tabs:

  • All: every dish.
  • Mine: dishes you added.
  • Hidden: dishes you hid. Tap Unhide to bring one back.
  • Allergies: dishes left out because of your allergens.
  • Excluded: dishes left out because they need an excluded ingredient.

The ⋮ button next to a dish lets you Like or Unlike it and hide it; your own dishes can also be edited or deleted.

Add your own dish

  1. In Settings → Menu, tap Add dish.
  2. Enter the Name and, if you like, the prep time in minutes.
  3. Choose the taste (Sweet or Savory), the products groups, and the allergens it contains.
  4. Type the Ingredients separated by commas, for example “Bread, Peanut butter”, and the Steps, one per line.
  5. Tap Add dish (or Save). Your dish now appears in the Cookbook, on Home when it fits, in suggestions and in the week plan.

13. Settings: Kitchen

Tell Fridge Pick which appliances you have, and recipes adapt to them. Open Settings → Kitchen and switch on what you can use: Stove, Oven, Steam cooker, Microwave, Toaster, Blender.

  • When a dish can be made in several ways, the dish page and recipe card show a method you can use. The chip under the dish name tells you which, for example “Steamed · steam over oven”.
  • Prefer steam cooker over oven: when both work (eggs, rice and beans, oatmeal…), the steam method is chosen.
  • The microwave is off by default and never used unless you switch it on.
  • Dishes you can’t cook with your appliances are left out of Home, I’m hungry suggestions and Fill week.

14. Settings: Units

Recipe amounts, oven temperatures and lengths can show in metric or in US customary units. The recipes themselves stay metric; only the display changes, including on a shared recipe card. Nutrition stays in grams. Open Settings and find Units (Единицы on a Russian phone). The choice is saved on the phone, and an open recipe updates when you change it.

  • Auto (Авто) is the default. It follows the phone’s measurement setting when iOS or Android reports one. A metric setting shows grams, milliliters and °C. A US, imperial or UK measurement setting shows ounces, cups and °F. When the phone does not report a measurement system, the United States, Liberia and Myanmar use imperial, and every other region uses metric. The line under the heading says which, for example “Follows your phone · grams, milliliters and °C” (“Как на телефоне · граммы, миллилитры и °C”).
  • Metric (Метрические): grams, milliliters and °C (Граммы, миллилитры и °C).
  • Imperial (Имперские): ounces, cups and °F (Унции, чашки и °F).

A line that already gives both, such as 180°C (360°F) or 1 cup (240 ml), keeps the side that matches your choice. Counts, pinches, “to taste”, and spoon measures already written as tsp or tbsp stay as written.

15. Settings: Allergies

Choose your allergens and intolerances from 11 common ones: Gluten, Dairy / lactose, Eggs, Peanuts, Tree nuts, Soy, Fish, Shellfish, Sesame, Celery and Mustard.

  1. Open Settings → Allergies.
  2. Tap everything you avoid, or None.
  3. Decide on Hide dishes with my allergens. On (default): those dishes disappear from the Cookbook. Off: they stay visible, dimmed, with a red warning badge.
  4. Tap Save.

Whatever you choose, dishes with your allergens are never suggested, planned or shopped for. If only an optional extra contains your allergen, the dish stays and the page tells you to skip that extra. You can also switch hiding on and off with the Safe for me chip on the Cookbook.

Allergen information is a convenience based on typical recipes and may be incomplete. Always check product labels. Fridge Pick is not medical advice.

16. Settings: Excluded ingredients

Never cook with olives, mushrooms or fish? Exclude them. Unlike allergies, an excluded ingredient isn’t treated as dangerous: it’s just something you don’t want to use.

Exclude an ingredient

  1. Open Settings → Excluded ingredients.
  2. Search for a product or type any ingredient in Search or type an ingredient, then tap it to exclude it.
  3. Or, on a dish page, long-press an ingredient (or use ⋮ → Exclude an ingredient…) and choose Never use …. The sheet shows how many dishes need it.
  • Dishes that need an excluded ingredient are never suggested, planned or added to the shopping list.
  • If it’s only an optional extra, the dish stays and the extra is left out (the recipe step says “skip it”).
  • If a line has alternatives, like “Butter or oil”, the dish still works with the other option.
  • Some words cover a family: “fish” also covers tuna, salmon and sardines; “cheese” covers feta and mozzarella.
  • Hide those dishes on home (on by default) hides them from the Cookbook; you’ll still find them in the Menu’s Excluded tab. The Without excluded chip on the Cookbook toggles this too.
  • To undo, tap Allow next to the ingredient, or Allow … again on a dish page.

17. Your data and offline use

Fridge Pick works fully offline and never connects to the internet. There’s no account, and nothing you enter (fridge products, dish history, favorites, week plan, shopping list, allergies, kitchen, units and other settings) leaves your device.

To delete everything, uninstall the app. On Android you can also go to Settings → Apps → Fridge Pick → Storage → Clear storage. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

18. FAQ

Do I need an account to use Fridge Pick?

No. There is no sign-up or sign-in. Open the app and start using it right away.

Does Fridge Pick work offline?

Yes. All dishes, recipes and photos are built into the app, and it never connects to the internet. You only need a connection to install or update it from the App Store or Google Play.

Which languages does the app support?

Most of the app is in English. Home, the tab bar, fruit names, the units setting and the seasonal line follow the phone’s language: Russian when the phone is set to Russian, and English otherwise. This website is in English.

Why don’t I see some dishes in the Cookbook?

A dish is left off the Cookbook when you hid it, when it contains one of your allergens (with Hide dishes with my allergens on), when it needs an ingredient you excluded, or when a filter is on. Seasonal keeps only dishes tagged for the current season, so all-year dishes stay off that chip. Tap Clear filters if nothing matches, and check the tabs in Settings → Menu to see every dish and why it isn’t shown. Home uses the same allergy, exclusion and kitchen rules, and it also skips dishes you cooked today or yesterday.

How do I get a hidden dish back?

Open Settings → Menu, go to the Hidden tab and tap Unhide next to the dish. Right after hiding a dish you can also tap Undo.

What counts as “Quick”?

The Quick filter shows dishes that take about 5 minutes or less. All built-in dishes take 15 minutes or less.

What do the badges on a dish mean?

A dish you cooked in the last 14 days, including day 14, shows Cooked today, Cooked yesterday or Cooked N days ago. On a Russian phone those lines are Готовили сегодня, Готовили вчера and Готовили N дня or дней назад. A dish you have never cooked, or last cooked longer ago than that, has no badge. Tap I made this on a dish page to update it.

How do I switch between metric and imperial?

Open Settings and choose Units: Auto, Metric or Imperial. Auto follows the phone’s measurement settings, or the region when the phone does not report them. The United States, Liberia and Myanmar use imperial in that fallback; every other region uses metric. A UK measurement setting uses imperial (ounces, cups and °F). Nutrition stays in grams. On a Russian phone the heading is Единицы, and the choices are Авто, Метрические and Имперские.

What happened to the Fruits item in My fridge?

Fresh fruit is now its own Fruits section (Фрукты on a Russian phone), with a chip for each fruit. There is no generic Fruits item. If you had Fruits ticked, the next launch ticks Apple, Banana, Orange, Berries, Grapes, Melon and Watermelon instead, and removes only that old tick. A dish such as fruit salad or yoghurt with fruit accepts any suitable fruit you have. The dish page shows that line as Any fruit.

What does “In season” mean?

On Home, the line under the hero reads In season when the season is why that dish is ahead of the next one. On a Russian phone it reads По сезону. The Seasonal filter on the Cookbook keeps dishes tagged for the current season. The hemisphere comes from the phone’s region. A dish is not hidden on Home just for being out of season.

Why don’t the recipes use a microwave?

The microwave is switched off in Settings → Kitchen by default, so recipes use your stove, steam cooker, oven, toaster or blender instead. You can switch it on, but Fridge Pick still prefers other methods when you have the right appliance.

How accurate are the calories and nutrition values?

They are approximate values per serving for a typical version of each recipe: calories, protein, carbs and fat. Your portion and products may differ.

Can I rely on the allergen labels?

Please don’t rely on them alone. Allergen markers are a convenience based on typical recipes and may be incomplete. Ingredients and brands vary, so always check the product labels. The app is not medical advice.

Can I use my data on another phone?

There are no accounts and no sync, so your fridge, plan, lists and settings stay on the device where you entered them. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) may include app data, depending on your system settings.

How do I delete my data?

You can clear parts of it in the app: Clear all in My fridge, Clear week in the week plan, Reset in the shopping list. To delete everything at once, uninstall the app. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Fridge Pick → Storage → Clear storage.

Most are from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licenses, the rest from Unsplash. Authors and licenses are listed on the Photo Credits page.
