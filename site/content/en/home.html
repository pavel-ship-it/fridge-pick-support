What is Fridge Pick?

Fridge Pick is a small recipe and meal-planning app for the moment you open the fridge and don’t know what to eat. It comes with 28 quick dishes, from oatmeal, omelettes and toasties to Greek salad and salmon with rice, each ready in 15 minutes or less. Every dish has a photo, a step-by-step recipe, approximate nutrition per serving and its common allergens.

Tell the app which products you have, and it shows which dishes you can make right now and what’s missing. Tell it about your allergies, the ingredients you never cook with and the appliances in your kitchen, and it only suggests dishes that suit you. Everything happens on your phone: there is no account, no internet connection and no data collection.

How it works

  1. Tick what you have. Open My fridge and tap the products you have at home. Salt, pepper, oil and water count as always there.
  2. See what you can cook. Turn on Can make now, or look at the hint on each dish: “Can make now”, “Missing: tomato” or “You have 4/7”.
  3. Cook and remember. Follow the recipe, then tap I made this. Fridge Pick remembers when you last had each dish and mixes things up.

    Answer up to four quick questions (time, hunger, craving, your fridge) and get 3–5 dishes with a reason for each.

    Plan the next 7 days with up to 3 dishes a day. Fill week adds a varied dish to every day; remove one and get 3 replacements.

    Plan your week
  • 🛒

    Shopping list

    Built from your week plan and the items you add from dish pages. Products already in your fridge are set aside. Share it as a text checklist.

    Shopping list guide
  • 📤

    Send any dish as a picture card with the photo, time, nutrition, ingredients and steps, plus the recipe as text.

    Share a recipe

    Set up allergies

Private and offline by design

  • Works fully offline. All dishes, recipes and photos are built in, and the app never connects to the internet.
  • No account, sign-up or sign-in.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. Your fridge, plan, lists and settings stay on your device.
